# Power BI Sales and Profit Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing sales, revenue, profit, expenses, and product performance.

The report contains four pages: Home Page, Map, Sub-Category Performance, and Decomposition Tree.
![Power BI Sales and Profit Dashboard](Power%20Bi%20Dashboard%20SS.png)

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Home Page
- Key performance indicators: total orders, expenses, revenue, and profit margin
- Revenue by product category
- Monthly revenue and profit trends
- Profit by sales method
- Cost versus price analysis by sub-category
- City filters

### 2. Map
- Revenue by city on a map
- City revenue comparison using a treemap
- Profit by city and category

### 3. Sub-Category Performance
- Product and sub-category details
- Total quantity sold
- Total revenue, expenses, and profit

### 4. Decomposition Tree
- Breaks down total profit by city, sales method, and product
- Includes a Key Influencers visual to explore factors associated with profit
